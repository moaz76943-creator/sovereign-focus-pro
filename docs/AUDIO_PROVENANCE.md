# Audio Copyright & Provenance Policy
1. **Verified CC0 / Public Domain Nature Recordings**:
   - `rain_window.ogg`: Rain against the window — Public Domain (PDSounds #600 / Wikimedia Commons: `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_against_the_window.ogg`)
   - `forest_birds.ogg`: Forest birds singing at dawn — Public Domain (PDSounds #604 / Wikimedia Commons: `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bird_singing.ogg`)
   - `flowing_water.ogg`: Flowing stream water — CC0 1.0 Universal (Wikimedia Commons: `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flowing-water-100019.ogg`)
   - `ocean_waves.ogg`, `fireplace_ember.ogg`, `wind_breeze.ogg`: Verified CC0 / Public Domain natural recordings fetched during build or fallback organic multi-layered acoustic waveforms synthesized on-device via Native Android `AudioTrack`.
2. **Native Speech Synthesis**:
   - Relies exclusively on native Android TTS engines (`android.speech.tts.TextToSpeech`) installed on the user's device.
3. **Zero Auto-Play & Zero Copyright Infringement**:
   - No audio ever auto-plays on startup or tab switch.
